# Teams Basics

## What is Microsoft Teams?

**Microsoft Teams** is a collaboration and communication platform used for:

* Chat
* Team communication
* Meetings
* Voice/video calls
* File sharing
* Collaborative document editing
* Calendar management
* Project collaboration

In a workplace, Teams can be used for communication between employees, project teams, managers, clients, and other stakeholders.

---

# Important Terms

## Team

A **Team** is a workspace for a group of people working together.

Example:

```text
Team: Project Alpha

Members:
- Developer
- Tester
- Manager
- Business Analyst
```

A Team can contain multiple channels.

---

## Channel

A **Channel** organizes conversations, files, and collaboration around a specific topic or workstream.

Example:

```text
Project Alpha
│
├── General
├── Development
├── Testing
└── Deployment
```

### Easy memory

> **Team = larger workspace**
> **Channel = topic/workstream inside a Team**

---

# Important Keyboard Shortcuts

Teams shortcuts can vary somewhat by platform and version, but these are common shortcuts worth knowing.

| Shortcut           | Action                                               |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `Ctrl + E`         | Search                                               |
| `Ctrl + N`         | Start a new chat/message in supported configurations |
| `Ctrl + Shift + M` | Toggle microphone                                    |
| `Ctrl + Shift + O` | Toggle camera                                        |
| `Ctrl + Shift + K` | Start a new meeting in supported configurations      |
| `Ctrl + Shift + A` | Accept incoming call                                 |
| `Ctrl + Shift + D` | Decline incoming call                                |
| `Ctrl + Shift + R` | Start/reply to message in supported contexts         |
| `Ctrl + .`         | Show keyboard shortcuts                              |
| `Esc`              | Close/exit current dialog or action                  |

### ⭐ Most important

For OA preparation, focus on:

```text
Ctrl + E            Search
Ctrl + Shift + M    Microphone
Ctrl + Shift + O    Camera
Ctrl + .            Show shortcuts
```

> **Important:** Microsoft Teams has changed its interface and shortcut behavior over time. If a question asks about a shortcut, pay attention to whether it specifies desktop/web and the Teams version.

# Chat

Teams Chat is used for direct communication.

## One-to-One Chat

Communication between two people.

Example:

```text
Rahul ↔ Priya
```

---

## Group Chat

Communication among multiple people.

Example:

```text
Rahul
Priya
Amit
Neha
```

All participants can communicate in the same chat.

---

# Chat Features

You can typically:

* Send text
* Send files
* Share links
* Send images
* Use emojis/reactions
* Mention people
* Start calls
* Start meetings
* Reply to messages
* Search messages

---

# Teams vs Chat

This is an important distinction.

| Chat                           | Team                                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Direct communication           | Structured workspace                |
| One-to-one or group            | Organization around a group/project |
| Conversation-focused           | Collaboration-focused               |
| Useful for quick communication | Useful for ongoing project work     |

### Easy memory

```text
Chat → Talk
Team → Work together
```

---

# Teams and Channels

A Team can have several channels.

Example:

```text
Team: Software Project

Channels:
├── General
├── Development
├── Testing
├── Documentation
└── Deployment
```

Each channel can contain:

* Posts/conversations
* Files
* Meetings
* Shared resources
* Tabs/apps

---

# General Channel

Many Teams have a **General** channel intended for broad team communication.

It can be used for:

* General announcements
* Team-wide discussions
* Information relevant to most members

---

# Private vs Standard Channels

## Standard Channel

Accessible to members of the Team according to the channel's permissions.

Useful for general project discussions.

---

## Private Channel

Restricted to specifically selected members.

Useful when information should be accessible only to a subset of the Team.

Example:

```text
Team: Project Alpha

Standard:
Development

Private:
Management
```

### Easy memory

```text
Standard → Team-wide access according to membership
Private  → Restricted membership
```

---

# Shared Channels

Microsoft Teams can also support **shared channels**, depending on the organization's configuration.

Shared channels are designed for collaboration with people who may not be members of the entire Team.

For an OA, remember the broad distinction:

```text
Standard → General team collaboration
Private  → Restricted group
Shared   → Collaboration across selected people/teams
```

Availability and permissions can depend on organizational settings.

# Scheduling a Meeting

A Teams meeting can be scheduled through Teams Calendar and, depending on the Microsoft 365 environment, Outlook.

A meeting typically contains:

```text
Meeting title
Date
Time
Participants
Agenda/details
Meeting link
```

---

# Meeting Organizer

The **organizer** is the person who schedules/creates the meeting.

The organizer may have additional controls, depending on meeting settings and policies.

---

# Meeting Participants

Participants are people who join the meeting.

They may be able to:

* Use microphone
* Use camera
* Chat
* Raise hand
* React
* Share content, if permitted

---

# Meeting Roles

Depending on meeting configuration, Teams can have roles such as:

* Organizer
* Co-organizer
* Presenter
* Attendee

Permissions differ by role.

---

## Organizer

Creates and manages the meeting.

---

## Co-organizer

Can assist the organizer with meeting management when assigned the appropriate role.

---

## Presenter

Can have additional capabilities such as presenting content.

---

## Attendee

Usually has more limited permissions.

### Easy memory

```text
Organizer → Controls meeting
Presenter → Presents
Attendee  → Participates
```

---

Microphone

Screen sharing

More options

# Microphone

The microphone control lets you:

```text
Mute
Unmute
```

### Why mute?

When you are not speaking, muting reduces background noise.

---

# Camera

The camera control lets you turn your video:

```text
ON
OFF
```

---

# Screen Sharing

Screen sharing allows you to show content from your computer to meeting participants.

You may be able to share:

* Entire screen
* Application/window
* PowerPoint presentation
* Other supported content

---

# Screen vs Window

## Share Screen

Shows the selected screen/display.

Potentially includes everything visible on that screen.

---

## Share Window

Shares only a selected application window.

Example:

```text
Share:
Microsoft PowerPoint
```

instead of showing your entire desktop.

### Important

When sharing sensitive information, verify exactly what you are sharing before presenting.

---

# PowerPoint Live

Teams can support presenting PowerPoint content using **PowerPoint Live**, depending on the meeting environment.

It can provide presentation-specific capabilities such as:

* Presenter view
* Slide navigation
* Presentation controls
* Audience experience improvements

For an OA, remember:

> **PowerPoint Live = presentation-focused sharing in Teams**

---

# Raise Hand

The **Raise Hand** feature allows participants to indicate that they want to speak without interrupting the current speaker.

Example:

```text
Meeting
   ↓
Raise Hand
   ↓
Host sees request
   ↓
Participant speaks
```

Useful during:

* Classes
* Presentations
* Team meetings
* Q&A sessions

---

# Meeting Chat

Meeting Chat allows participants to exchange text during a meeting.

It can be used to:

* Ask questions
* Share links
* Share information
* Discuss meeting topics

Depending on meeting settings, chat availability may differ before, during, or after a meeting.

---

# Reactions

Participants can use reactions to communicate nonverbally.

Examples can include:

```text
👍
❤️
😂
👏
😮
```

Reactions are useful when you want to acknowledge something without interrupting the speaker.

---

# Recording

Teams can support meeting recording depending on organizational permissions and policies.

A recording can allow authorized participants to review a meeting later.

### Important

Recording availability and access can depend on:

* Organization settings
* User permissions
* Meeting type
* Microsoft 365 configuration

Do not assume every meeting can automatically be recorded.

---

# Transcription

Teams may support live or post-meeting transcription depending on configuration.

Transcription converts spoken content into text.

Useful for:

* Meeting notes
* Accessibility
* Reviewing discussions
* Finding information later

---

# Files and Collaboration

Teams supports file sharing and collaboration.

Files can be shared through:

* Chat
* Channels
* Meetings
* Tabs/apps

---

# Collaborative Editing

Microsoft 365 documents can often be edited collaboratively.

Examples:

```text
Word
Excel
PowerPoint
```

Multiple users can work on supported files together.

### Example

```text
Rahul → edits Word document
Priya → edits same document
Amit  → reviews document
```

Changes can be synchronized through Microsoft 365 collaboration features.

---

# Channel Files

Files uploaded to a channel are associated with that channel's file storage.

This allows team members to access project documents from a central location, subject to permissions.

---

# Chat File Sharing vs Channel Files

### Chat

Useful for sharing a file directly with people involved in a conversation.

### Channel

Useful for storing and collaborating on files related to a Team/workstream.

### Easy memory

```text
Chat   → Conversation-specific sharing
Channel → Team/project collaboration
```

---

# Presence and Status

Teams displays user availability through presence indicators.

Common statuses include:

```text
Available
Busy
Do not disturb
Be right back
Away
Offline
```

---

## Available

Indicates that the person is generally available.

---

## Busy

Indicates that the person is busy.

---

## Do Not Disturb

Indicates that the person does not want interruptions.

Notifications may be restricted according to settings.

---

## Be Right Back

Indicates temporary absence.

---

## Away

Indicates that the person is away/not currently active.

---

## Offline

Indicates that the person is not currently connected/available through Teams.

---

# Status vs Activity

Your presence can be influenced by factors such as:

* Calendar events
* Activity
* Manual status selection
* Device/network state

The exact behavior can depend on Teams settings.

---

# Notifications

* New messages
* Mentions

* Replies

Notification settings can usually be customized.

# Mentions

Mentions are used to notify a specific person or group.

Example:

```text

```

The mentioned user receives a notification according to their notification settings.

---

# Types of Mentions

Depending on the Teams environment, mentions may include:

```text

@channel
```

Availability and behavior can depend on organization settings.

### Example

```text
@Rahul
```

targets Rahul.

# Breakout Rooms

Breakout rooms allow meeting participants to be divided into smaller groups.

Example:

```text
Main Meeting
│
├── Room 1 → Team A
├── Room 2 → Team B
└── Room 3 → Team C
```

Useful for:

* Workshops
* Group discussions
* Training
* Brainstorming

# Commonly Confused Concepts

## Team vs Channel

```text
Team    → Workspace/group
Channel → Topic/workstream inside a Team
```

---

## Chat vs Channel

```text
Chat    → Direct/group conversation
Channel → Structured team/project collaboration
```

---

## Standard vs Private Channel

```text
Standard → Broad Team access
Private  → Restricted members
```

---

## Organizer vs Presenter vs Attendee

```text
Organizer → Manages meeting
Presenter → Presents content
Attendee  → Participates
```

---

## Screen Share vs Window Share

```text
Screen → Share selected display
Window → Share selected application window
```

---

## Mute vs Camera Off

```text
Mute        → Audio off
Camera Off  → Video off
```

---

## Flag vs Teams Status

These are different concepts.

```text
Outlook Flag → Follow-up/reminder
Teams Status → Availability/presence
```
